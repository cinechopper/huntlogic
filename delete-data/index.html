Delete your Hunt Logic data

Hunt Logic has no accounts. Your stands, pins, tracks and journal are stored only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them.

How to request deletion

  1. Email huntlogic.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hunt Logic data".
  2. Include the email address you signed up with (if you joined the email list) and the name you used in Hunt Party (if any).
  3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

You can also do most of this yourself: leave your Hunt Party in the app to remove your party profile and location, and use the unsubscribe link in any email to leave the list.

What is deleted

  • Email sign-up: your email address and sign-up details.
  • Hunt Party: your party profile (name and optional photo), your shared locations, your pins and deer marks, and your notification token.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Hunt Party locations delete themselves after about 4 hours, and pins and marks after about 18 hours, even if you don't ask.
  • Anonymous deer sightings you chose to share are stored without any name, device or account and only as a rough area, so they can't be traced back to you and are kept to improve movement forecasts.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; manage or cancel Hunt Logic Pro there.
