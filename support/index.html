Common questions

How do I manage or cancel Hunt Logic Pro?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hunt Logic. You can also open Hunt Logic → Settings → Membership → Manage subscription.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.
In Hunt Logic, go to Settings → Membership → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

What's included for free?
Maps, public land and property lines, pins and tracks, up to three planned stands with their leave times, a 3-day movement outlook, the journal, recovery trail marking, radar, CWD zones and the full Hunt Party. Free also includes one Scout Scan.

Does the map work without signal?
Yes, for areas you save offline (Map → ⋯ → Save area offline). Forecasts shown offline are from the last time you had signal and don't update in the field.

Who can see my location in Hunt Party?
Only people with your party code, and only while you have sharing turned on. Turn it off or leave the party any time.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
