What it does

  • Scout Scan. Likely bedding, saddles, pinches, draws and travel corridors, with the reason for each.
  • No more stand math. A leave time worked back from legal light through your drive, walk and setup.
  • Wind, thermals and scent. See where your scent goes, from you or your stand.
  • Deer movement forecast. Morning and evening outlooks with stand freshness.
  • Hunt Party. Share locations and sightings with your group. Free for everyone.
  • Journal and recovery. Sightings log themselves; mark blood and sign on a trail map.

Free and Pro

Free includes maps, public land and property lines, up to 3 planned stands with leave times, a 3-day movement outlook, the journal, recovery marking, one Scout Scan, two deer movement simulations and the full Hunt Party. Pro adds unlimited scans and stands ranked for each sit, the full forecast, advanced map layers, thermals and journal analysis. Beta testers get all of Pro free.
