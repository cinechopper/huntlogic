HUNT LOGIC

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Hunt Logic is a hunting planner for iPhone. It is built so that your hunting spots stay yours: there is no Hunt Logic account, no Hunt Logic server holding your data, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your stands, pins, sightings, recorded tracks, journal, recoveries, photos and settings are stored on your iPhone. We never receive them. If you export a backup or share a stand, you choose where that file goes.

Your location

Hunt Logic uses your location to show you on the map, measure distance to your stands, time your drive and walk in, and — if you allow "Always" — to keep timing a trip in the background and notice when you leave your stand. Your location is used on your phone and is not sent to us.

Services that receive a map area or coordinates

To draw maps and fetch conditions, the app asks outside services for data about an area. Those requests include the coordinates of the area being viewed or planned, as any map app's requests do:

  • Mapbox — map tiles and satellite imagery.
  • Apple — Apple Maps routing, place search and directions.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts.
  • U.S. government and public data services (such as USGS, USDA, NOAA and public-land databases) — elevation, water, crops, soil, radar and public land.
  • Google Maps — only if you choose to open directions in Google Maps.

These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. None of them receives your name, your journal or your stands as records.

Hunt Party

Hunt Party is optional. When you join a party and turn on sharing, your display name, your location, and the sightings and paths you choose to share are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so the other members of your party can see them. Anyone who has your party code can see what is shared in that party. Location updates are sent only while sharing is on and expire after a short time. Turn sharing off or leave the party at any time.

Purchases

Hunt Logic Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app receives only the subscription status Apple provides, to turn Pro features on.

Notifications

Reminders such as when to leave are scheduled on your phone. Party alerts are delivered through Apple's push service.

Children

Hunt Logic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your phone, deleting the app deletes it. Hunt Party records you shared expire on their own; leaving the party stops any new sharing.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: huntlogic.support@gmail.com.
