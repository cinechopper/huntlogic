What we ask of you

  • You really hunt deer. Whitetail, mule deer or blacktail, bow, gun or muzzleloader. Public land, private, small farm, big woods, all welcome.
  • Make at least one video in your first 30 days, then as many as you like. Short or long, your style. You keep your content and your voice.
  • Say it is a free app from Hunt Logic. In every video, see the box below.
  • Be honest. If something does not work for your hunting, say so. We would rather hear it than have you fake it. Do not claim the app will find you a deer, or that it replaces scouting, woodsmanship or local rules.
  • Hunt legally and safely. Follow your state's laws and basic safety, and no content that shows either broken.

Disclosure, required in every video

The FTC requires you to say when you got something free in return for a video. Do both of these, every time:

1. In the video: say it out loud or put it on screen near the start: "Hunt Logic gave me this app for free."

2. In the caption: put #ad or "Free app from Hunt Logic" where it shows before the "more" cut-off, plus the platform's own paid-partnership or branded-content label where it has one.

"Thanks Hunt Logic" or burying it in a block of hashtags is not enough. Free Pro counts, even with no cash involved.

What a good video looks like

Real use on a real hunt, not an ad read. Show the screen while you do something with it: pick a stand for tomorrow's wind, plan a shot angle, send a pin to your hunting buddy. Say what surprised you and what you would still do yourself. Thirty to ninety seconds on short-form is plenty. Longer is fine as a section of a regular hunt video.

Three ideas to start:

  1. Which stand tomorrow?Open the wind forecast for two stands you hunt and show which one Hunt Logic picks. Then tell us if you agree, and what you did.
  2. Plan a shot before you drawUse Plan a shot to see where the deer's vitals sit from your stand height and distance. Show what changed your mind about a 30-yard quartering-away shot.
  3. Hunt Party with the crewShare one sighting with a buddy on another phone, iPhone and Android, and show how it appears on both maps.

Questions

Does "free for life" really mean free forever?

For as long as you stay in the program, yes: you get a free year of Pro at a time and we renew it each year. We do this with App Store offer codes on iPhone and Google Play promo codes on Android, so there is no payment on your end. If we ever end the program we will tell you in writing first.

Do I need 2,000 followers?

We are starting with channels from about 2,000 to 150,000 on any one platform. If you are a little under and your hunting content is good, apply anyway and say so.

Do you pay for views or installs?

No. There is no cash payment today. We count installs from your personal link so we can learn what works, and we may offer a commission later. Nothing here promises one.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. We do not write scripts or approve videos. We will only ask you to fix something that is plainly wrong about what the app does, or missing the disclosure.

Do I have to hide that I am a creator for Hunt Logic?

The opposite. You must say it. That is the rule that keeps your audience's trust, and it is the law.

Android or iPhone?

Both work. Hunt Logic is on the App Store and Google Play.

How do I stop hearing from you?

Email huntlogic.support@gmail.com and say so. We will not contact you again.
