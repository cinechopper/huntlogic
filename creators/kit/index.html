Talking points

In plain words, so you can say it your way.

  • What it is: a deer hunting planner for your phone. It does the stand math: which stand fits the wind, when to leave so you are set up before shooting light, and where deer are likely to move.
  • Free to download on the App Store and Google Play. Pro unlocks the full toolkit and is a yearly subscription with a free trial. You have Pro free as a creator, so say so.
  • Scout Scan marks likely bedding, travel corridors and pinch points from terrain and cover. It is a starting point for scouting, not proof of where deer are. Confirm in the field.
  • Plan a shot / Read a shot shows vitals on a deer at your distance, stand height and weapon, and helps you think through a shot and a recovery. It does not take the place of knowing your own effective range or of tracking skills.
  • Hunt Party lets your crew share pins, sightings and, if everyone turns it on, live location. It works between iPhone and Android.
  • Works for any hunter from a first-year bowhunter to a guy with 20 years on the same farm. Do not talk down to either.
  • What it is not: it does not guarantee a deer, it does not replace local regulations, and its predictions are estimates. Say that plainly, it makes you more credible.
  • Do not compare it to other hunting apps by name, put other companies down, or promise results. Show what it did for you.

10 video ideas

Each one is matched to a feature, so you are showing something real. Pick what fits your hunting.

  1. Plan a shotThe 30-yard quartering-away testSet the angle, distance and your weapon, and see where the aim point lands. Would you take it? What does the app say?
  2. Plan a shotShort and steep: what a tree stand changesPlan a shot from 20 feet up at 15, 20 and 30 yards. Show how the aim point moves and what it means for your next practice session.
  3. Stand math, windWhich stand tomorrow?Put in two stands and the forecast wind, and show which one fits. Hunt it, and report back what you saw.
  4. Stand math, thermalsMorning vs evening thermals on my hillsideHilly ground? Show how the air is likely to move at your set and whether you would change your approach.
  5. Stand math, leave timeThe alarm planLet it build your departure time from drive, walk and set-up, then follow it. Did it get you in without bumping deer?
  6. Scout ScanScout Scan vs my own boots-on-the-groundRun it on a new piece of public land, walk it, and show what it got right and wrong.
  7. Scout ScanFinding a pinch point with a map and a phoneUse Scout Scan to pick three spots for a weekend, then tell us how you ranked them.
  8. Hunt PartyHunting with the crew, two phones, one mapShare a sighting between an iPhone and an Android and show it on both maps. Good for camp videos.
  9. Recovery and blood trackingReading the shot before you leave the standUse Read a shot with a real or practice shot to talk through your wait time. Be careful, be respectful, and no gore.
  10. Recovery and blood trackingBlood on the arrow: what do you actually see?Check my arrow and Check this blood work from a photo you choose. Try it on a practice or past recovery and tell us how close it was to what you found. It is a second opinion, not the call.

Your free-access code

Your code is yours alone. Please do not post it. Apple's codes work for accounts that have not had Hunt Logic Pro, or whose Pro has ended. If you are already subscribed, tell us and we will sort out the right way.
