Hunting around Gallatin

Gallatin is the Daviess County seat and sits just west of the Grand River, which flows past about a mile east of town. That means a lot of the hunting nearby is river-bottom ground: crop fields running up to wooded banks, sloughs, and timber that floods in wet years. North and west you get rolling pasture, CRP and wooded draws. The Grindstone Fork and the Adam-ondi-Ahman area are close by.

We also take calls from Jamesport, Jameson, Winston, Altamont and the rest of Daviess County. It's our home county, so a Gallatin call is one of our shortest drives.

Gallatin is about 20 miles by road from our base in Pattonsburg.

Missouri rules

  • Since September 2024, drones may locate and recover deer, turkey, elk and bear. Never to find game for hunting, chase or harass it.
  • Landowner permission first. Conservation areas need a conservation agent's OK.
  • No weapons in the recovery party while the drone is up, except a concealed handgun. Leave the bow or rifle at the truck.

How a recovery works around Gallatin

A deer hit near the river often heads for the thickest cover it can find, and bottomland timber with downed trees is miserable to grid-search in the dark. From a few hundred feet up, the thermal camera can scan a whole bottom, a fence line, or the edge of a cut bean field in minutes, and the zoom camera checks each heat signature so we're not sending you after a coon or a coyote.

If the deer is still alive and bedded, we tell you where, and you decide how to follow up. We don't fly low to push it.

Questions

How far do you travel from Pattonsburg to Gallatin?

Gallatin is about 20 miles by road from Pattonsburg, and all of Daviess County is in our regular service area.

Can a thermal drone find a deer in the Grand River bottoms?

Often, yes. Thermal shows body heat through cornstalks, CRP and open timber. Thick leafy canopy, flooded brush and warm afternoons make it harder, so we're honest about the odds when you call.

Do I need the landowner's permission?

Yes. Missouri requires landowner permission for drone recovery, and a conservation agent's permission on conservation areas. Tell us who owns the ground when you call.

What should I text you?

Your county, the nearest road or landmark, the time of the shot, bow or gun, and whether you have landowner permission. The Text us button fills in a template.
