Hunting around Maryville

Maryville is the Nodaway County seat, laid out on rolling prairie above the One Hundred and Two River. The Nodaway River forms the county's western edge. Most of the county is big crop ground, with timber along the rivers and creeks and CRP in the draws.

We also take calls from Pickering, Ravenwood, Hopkins, Barnard, Burlington Junction, Conception Junction and the rest of Nodaway County.

Maryville is about 60 miles by road from Pattonsburg, at the western edge of our area. We'll give you an honest arrival estimate on the phone.

Missouri rules

  • Since September 2024, drones may locate and recover deer, turkey, elk and bear. Never to find game for hunting, chase or harass it.
  • Landowner permission first. Conservation areas need a conservation agent's OK.
  • No weapons in the recovery party while the drone is up, except a concealed handgun. Leave the bow or rifle at the truck.

How a recovery works around Maryville

In open crop country a hit deer often makes it to the nearest strip of cover: a timbered creek, a grassed waterway, a fence row. From above, the drone can check every strip of cover around your field quickly, and it finds deer bedded in CRP that you could walk within ten yards of.

Because of the drive, call as soon as you know you'll need help. We'll ask the right questions and plan the flight on the way.

Questions

Do you really cover Maryville?

Yes. Nodaway County is in our service area. Maryville is about 60 miles by road from Pattonsburg, so call early.

Can thermal find a deer in CRP or tall grass?

Yes, that's one of the places thermal does best. A deer's body heat stands out against cool grass, especially at night.

Will the drone spook the deer?

We fly high and quiet. A bedded deer usually stays put, which is a big advantage over walking in on it.
