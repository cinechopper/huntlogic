Hunting around Trenton

Trenton is the Grundy County seat and the largest town on the Thompson River. The Weldon River joins the Thompson about two miles northwest of town, near Crowder State Park. Along both rivers you get bottomland crop fields and river timber. Up out of the bottoms it turns into rolling pasture, CRP and wooded draws.

We also cover Spickard, Laredo, Galt and the rest of Grundy County, plus Mercer County to the north.

Trenton is roughly 40 miles by road from Pattonsburg.

Missouri rules

  • Since September 2024, drones may locate and recover deer, turkey, elk and bear. Never to find game for hunting, chase or harass it.
  • Landowner permission first. Conservation areas need a conservation agent's OK.
  • No weapons in the recovery party while the drone is up, except a concealed handgun. Leave the bow or rifle at the truck.

How a recovery works around Trenton

A deer hit near the river timber often heads for the thickest bottom cover. A drone can follow the most likely escape routes from above, along the timber edge, down a waterway, across a cut field, without anyone walking in and jumping it.

Once we find it on thermal, the zoom camera checks it's a deer and not a hot stump or a coyote. Then we talk you to it.

Questions

How far is Trenton from Pattonsburg?

About 40 miles by road. Grundy and Mercer counties are both in our service area.

Can a drone find a deer in the Thompson River bottoms?

Often. Thermal works best in cool weather and at night, and once leaves start falling. Heavy canopy and warm days lower the odds.

Can you fly in Crowder State Park?

State parks and conservation areas have their own rules. On conservation areas Missouri requires a conservation agent's permission for drone recovery. Most of our calls are on private ground with the landowner's OK.

Do you guarantee you'll find my deer?

No one honestly can. A thermal drone finds deer that a grid search misses, but a poor hit or thick cover can beat any method.
