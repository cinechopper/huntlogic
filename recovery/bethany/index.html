Hunting around Bethany

Bethany is the Harrison County seat, right on I-35 between Kansas City and Des Moines. Big Creek forms on the west side of town where its East and West Forks come together, then runs south toward the Grand River. Around it is classic north Missouri hill country: rolling pasture and hay ground, CRP, crop fields on the ridges, and timbered creek draws in between.

We also cover Ridgeway, New Hampton, Eagleville, Gilman City, Cainsville and the rest of Harrison County, which borders Iowa to the north.

Bethany is roughly 20 miles by road from Pattonsburg, one of our closest towns.

Missouri rules

  • Since September 2024, drones may locate and recover deer, turkey, elk and bear. Never to find game for hunting, chase or harass it.
  • Landowner permission first. Conservation areas need a conservation agent's OK.
  • No weapons in the recovery party while the drone is up, except a concealed handgun. Leave the bow or rifle at the truck.

How a recovery works around Bethany

Deer in this country tend to drop off a ridge into the nearest wooded draw. On foot you're climbing in and out of creek banks with a flashlight. From above, the drone can work a whole draw and the fields on both sides in one pass, and thermal picks up a bedded deer that you would walk right past.

Because we're close, we can usually start the trip right after your call. Just tell us the nearest road and what you saw after the shot.

Questions

Do you cover Bethany and Harrison County?

Yes. Bethany is roughly 20 miles from our base in Pattonsburg, and we take calls across Harrison County, including Ridgeway, New Hampton, Eagleville and Gilman City.

How soon should I call after the shot?

Call as soon as your wait time is up, or right away if you aren't sure of the hit. We can talk through timing on the phone. Cool nights are when thermal works best.

Can you find a deer in a wooded creek draw?

Often. In fall, once leaves start dropping, thermal sees through open timber well. Thick cedar and heavy leaf cover can block the view, and we'll tell you that up front.

Can I bring my rifle along on the recovery?

No. Missouri rules say no one in the recovery party can carry a firearm or bow while the drone is flying, except a concealed handgun.
