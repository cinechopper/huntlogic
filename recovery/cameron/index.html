Hunting around Cameron

Cameron sits where Clinton, DeKalb and Caldwell counties meet, so a call from here could put us in any of the three. Shoal Creek runs through Clinton and Caldwell counties on its way to the Grand River, and the country around town is a mix of row crops, pasture, timbered creeks and draws. Grindstone Reservoir and Wallace State Park about seven miles south are the kind of wooded ground you see scattered through the area.

We also take calls from Osborn, Turney, Lathrop, Kidder, Hamilton and Maysville.

Cameron is about 25 miles by road from Pattonsburg, a straight run down the I-35 corridor.

Missouri rules

  • Since September 2024, drones may locate and recover deer, turkey, elk and bear. Never to find game for hunting, chase or harass it.
  • Landowner permission first. Conservation areas need a conservation agent's OK.
  • No weapons in the recovery party while the drone is up, except a concealed handgun. Leave the bow or rifle at the truck.

How a recovery works around Cameron

Around Cameron a deer can cross a road and a property line in a hurry. Before we fly, we confirm whose ground the deer is likely on and that you have permission for each piece. Then the drone works the edges: tree lines, waterways, the timber along a creek, standing corn. The zoom camera confirms each heat source before anyone walks in.

Public ground like the state park and conservation areas has its own rules. On conservation areas you need a conservation agent's permission before we fly.

Questions

Do you cover Cameron and Clinton County?

Yes. Cameron is about 25 miles by road from Pattonsburg, and we cover Clinton, DeKalb and Caldwell counties around it.

What if the deer crossed onto the neighbor's land?

We need permission from each landowner whose ground we search. Call them first, or tell us who they are and we'll work it out before we launch.

Can you search standing corn?

Thermal can pick up a deer down the rows in standing corn, especially at night. Tall, dense crops can hide a deer lying low, so we search the edges and waterways too.

Is drone deer recovery legal in Missouri?

Yes, since September 2024, for recovery only. Drones may never be used to find game to hunt, and no one in the party can carry a gun or bow while it flies, except a concealed handgun.
